A newsroom's reader, as an API.

A reading meter that charges by how far you read. Paying after you read, with a rating that sets the price. Personas, a newsroom of your own, reading lists in a link, encrypted sharing, a vault that carries your account between devices. All of it runs in the reader's browser, with no server of ours, and all of it is open source. It is live on newsroom.sgit.ai, and it is taking money.

The meter

Pay by depth, after you read

A page is charged by the deepest point reached; a rating at the foot sets the price from nothing to double. £5 of credit to start, in the reader's own browser.

 Personas

A newsroom of your own

Read as a founder, a journalist, a security lead, a builder or a board member; keep and reject articles; picks follow. A link can open the newsroom as a persona.

 Reading lists

From me to you, in a link

Every article has a number; a list is a link with the numbers in it. No account, no server, nothing stored but the link.

 The vault

Your account, between devices

A passkey opens a door; the door holds the key to a master vault; the account travels encrypted, and the newsroom never holds a key.

How it works, in one picture

The code is served from this host at a versioned path and never changed after it is published. The reader's state belongs to the page's origin, so it stays with the newsroom whichever host serves the script, and the newsroom never sees it. Nothing leaves the browser unless the reader pays, shares or subscribes, and each of those is a published contract. The hosting model, in full.

Open source, and why that is the point

The code is Apache 2.0, in one repository, with its tests, its release manifests, its review findings as issues, and the brief it was built to. A publisher can pin a release today and run it on their own pages; a developer can read every line; an evaluator can check every hash against the live host. Nothing here needs permission.

The newsroom that runs it is the reference, not the product. The aim is other newsrooms adopting the same reader, so that a reading list or a persona made on one site means something on another, and so that the formats, the article number, the list link, the share, the envelope, outlive any one publisher.
