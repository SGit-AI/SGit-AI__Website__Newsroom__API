The reading-list link

A reading list is a link. Everything the recipient sees is in the part after the #, which never reaches a server; nothing is stored anywhere but the link itself.
https://newsroom.sgit.ai/for/#from=Dinis&to=Steve&r=65,60,62&s=66&f=founder&n=Start%20with%2065
FieldWhat it isRules

What opens

The page reads the fragment, looks each number up in articles/ids.json, and shows the list with titles, dates and teasers from the feed. Opening it adds a visitor persona named for the list ("Steve's list, from Dinis") through the meter's public calls, so the reader can keep it as a persona of their own or remove it; nothing is charged for opening a list. A list's persona is keyed by a hash of the names and numbers (finding R17 notes the hash is 32 bits).

The make page

/for/make.html builds a link from a form: search the articles, add up to 50, move them up and down, name the sender and the reader, write the note, copy the link. It reads its own link back through the recipient's parser, so what the preview shows is what the link opens. An existing link loads into the form for editing.

What is deliberately not in it

  • A signature. The names are whatever the link says; a signed list is a later step on the newsroom's keys, and the page says so.
  • A server. No list is stored anywhere; a link is the whole list.
  • Paying for someone else's reading. Designed, not built.

For a site that adopts it

Publish articles/ids.json with a stable n for every article (the newsroom's admin/build/ids.py assigns numbers once, in publication order, and its gate refuses a change) and import for/reading-list.js and for/make.js from this host on two pages; the newsroom's for/index.html and for/make.html are the worked examples. The parser's limits are in LIMITS at the top of reading-list.js; the tests that hold them are tests/test_lists.mjs.

For agents. The reading-list link contract: /for/#from=&to=&r=&s=&f=&n=&e=; names up to 40 characters of letters, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes; r and s are article numbers (1 to 50 in all); f persona ids or c:<collection> (up to 10); n up to 240 characters; e an address; malformed fields are dropped and listed; the list opens as a visitor persona; no signature yet. This page as markdown: index.md. The site's index: llms.txt; the whole site in one fetch: llms-full.txt. The reader code this site describes is served at https://api.newsroom.sgit.ai/v0.2.5/<path>; its manifest: v0.2.5/manifest.json.

api.newsroom.sgit.ai

The SGit Newsroom's reader, as an API: the components, formats and contracts the browser runs on newsroom.sgit.ai, with no server and nothing to deploy but files, published here as releases a site pins.
